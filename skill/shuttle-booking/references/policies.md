# Booking policies

- Each booking is for one passenger and one seat.
- Seats are held for 10 minutes. An unpaid hold expires automatically when a later availability or hold request cleans up expired reservations.
- A payment link is valid only for the associated hold and fare.
- Payment is confirmed only by Paystack's server-side transaction verification or a valid signed Paystack webhook. A screenshot or client callback is not proof.
- The ticket reference is issued after successful payment verification.
- Booking lookup requires both the ticket reference and the phone number used for the booking.
- Seatline does not process refunds, cancellations, changes, or group reservations through this assistant; hand these requests to staff.
- Prices are listed as integer pesewas in the trip data; 100 pesewas equals GHS 1.00.

The sample route schedule and prices are fictional demonstration data.
